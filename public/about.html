About Me

My name is Mirza Hussain Alizadeh, and I am an aspiring Information and Communication Technologies student at Kateb University with a strong background in education and community service..

Over the past several years, I have worked as an English Instructor, a Professor Assistant, and a volunteer teacher, helping students develop their language skills, critical thinking, and confidence. These experiences have strengthened my communication, leadership, and organizational abilities while allowing me to work effectively with learners from diverse backgrounds..

I am fluent in Dari, Pashto, and English, and I have earned a TOEFL score of 90 and a Duolingo English Test score of 115. Alongside my teaching experience, I have completed training in Python programming and ICDL, which has expanded my technical skills and increased my interest in technology and digital innovation.

What motivates me most is using education and technology to create opportunities for others. I enjoy learning new skills, solving problems, and contributing to meaningful projects that have a positive impact on individuals and communities.

I consider myself a dedicated, adaptable, and lifelong learner who is always eager to grow both personally and professionally. I look forward to applying my knowledge, gaining new experiences, and making valuable contributions wherever I have the opportunity to serve.
